Heun functions
--------------

The general Heun equation has four regular singular points, at
:math:`0`, :math:`1`, :math:`a` and :math:`\infty`. It generalizes the Gauss
hypergeometric equation, which has three. [DLMF]_ section 31.7 lists
reductions to :func:`~mpmath.hyp2f1`.
